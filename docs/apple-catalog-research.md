# Apple catalog verification

Research date: **2026-09-05**  
Scope: Apple-owned primary sources only, using Apple’s U.S. product/store pages where available. “Current” means Apple has a live U.S. product or store listing on the research date. “Archive-only” means Apple documents the product in Support or comparison pages but does not list it in the current new-product store.

## Executive result

- **Current official products:** MacBook Air (13-inch and 15-inch, M5), MacBook Pro (14-inch and 16-inch, M5 family), MacBook Neo, Mac mini (M6 or M5 Pro; pre-order), iPhone 17, iPhone 17 Pro Max, iPhone 16, iPhone 16 Plus, Apple Watch Series 11, Apple Watch Ultra 3, Apple Watch SE 3, AirPods Pro 3, AirPods 4, AirPods Max 2, and the requested current accessories described below.
- **Official but archive-only:** iPhone 16 Pro, iPhone 16 Pro Max, iPhone 15 Pro Max, iPhone 14 Pro Max, and the Lightning-era MagSafe Battery Pack.
- **No Apple-authored product page found:** Apple Watch Series 12, Apple Watch Ultra 4, and AirPods 5. Do not seed these names or inferred specifications as official products. Apple’s current stores instead list Series 11 / Ultra 3 / SE 3 and AirPods 4 / Pro 3 / Max 2 ([Watch store](https://www.apple.com/shop/buy-watch), [AirPods lineup](https://www.apple.com/airpods/)). Apple Podcasts pages that happen to mention rumored names are third-party publisher content, not Apple product announcements.

## Catalog implementation notes

1. Seed only products marked **Current** if the goal is to mirror Apple’s current new-product store. Keep archive-only phones/battery pack behind an explicit “legacy” policy if they are sold by third-party sellers.
2. Model storage and finish as independent dimensions, but validate only combinations Apple actually offers for that model. Mac chip choice also constrains storage; it cannot safely be represented as a flat Cartesian product.
3. For Apple Watch, separate the watch case SKU from bands. For AirPods 4, the ANC choice is a model variant, not a color.
4. The image links above are Apple-hosted assets exposed in current page metadata/source on the research date. Apple can rotate versioned assets, and an accessible URL does not itself grant redistribution rights; confirm image-use rights before copying them into a production catalog.
